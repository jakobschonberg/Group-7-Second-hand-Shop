**Overall Team Contribution**

Although each team member was responsible for different user stories and had different Scrum roles, we all contributed to almost every part of the project. We reviewed each other's pull requests, discussed solutions, tested features, and helped fix bugs. This gave everyone a better understanding of the entire system, not just their own tasks. We worked together throughout the project and shared knowledge and responsibilities.
 
**2. One Review That Changed Something**
   
During a code review, we found that identifying duplicate data during product imports was difficult. We discussed the problem as a team and agreed to add a database constraint on the category to help prevent duplicate data.
This review helped us improve data consistency and avoid duplicate entries.

**3. One Difficult Problem and How We Solved It**
   
One challenge was merging code from different team members. Sometimes different settings, database paths, and functions caused problems when combining our work.
We solved these issues by reviewing the code together, discussing the problems, testing the merged program, and fixing the errors as a team.
This helped us understand each other's code better and improved our collaboration.

**4. What Worked Well in Our Scrum Process?**
   
Our communication and teamwork worked well throughout the project. Daily Scrum meetings helped us stay organized, share progress, and identify problems early.
Code reviews helped us find bugs before merging and learn from each other. Everyone supported each other, and we worked together to complete our user stories.
Having a clear daily work schedule also helped us manage our time and understand our responsibilities.

**5. One Thing We Would Do Differently Next Time**
   
Next time, team members who finish their tasks early should help others complete the remaining work instead of waiting.
We could work together on unfinished issues, review code, or help with testing to make sure the sprint is completed on time.
 
We also discussed how Scrum works in real-world projects and agreed that supporting each other when tasks are completed early could improve teamwork and efficiency.
